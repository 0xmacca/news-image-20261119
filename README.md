One image summing up the world's news of **Thursday, 19 November 2026**, in a
newspaper-infographic style: masthead with the date, the day's top story (the
first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen and its joint peace
declaration, with no breakthrough on Ukraine), and four more panels — the
World Court ruling Myanmar committed genocide, the Kerch Bridge hit a third
time, tankers ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz, and Anthropic's record IPO
debut — plus four more stories in the bottom strip. Every fact was
cross-checked against ten daily news briefs for 19 November 2026; the image
is drawn from scratch with code, no photographs.

![The world's news of 19 November 2026](entry.png)

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`, so two runs write byte-identical output. No network.
